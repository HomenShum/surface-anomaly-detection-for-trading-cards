# Three-Minute Demo Script

1. Open `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Click **Try sample card**.
3. Open the sample Pokémon report.
4. Point out the DING overlay, confidence, and required disclaimer.
5. Add a correction: mark one detection as glare or false positive.
6. Open **Cards** and show the saved catalog view.
7. Open **Compare** and show before/after candidate defects.
8. Open the evidence manifest section and point to hashes, timestamp, model version, and report version.

Close with:

Before you send your card anywhere, scan it, understand visible condition risk, and keep a timestamped evidence file.
