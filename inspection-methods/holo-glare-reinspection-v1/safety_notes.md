# Safety Notes

- Raw user images are not published in this registry.
- Derived crops may be stored in private evidence manifests only when the user creates a scan/report.
- Shared learning is limited to anonymized method outcomes and benchmark metrics.
- The method cannot claim an official grade or legal proof.
- The method is verified on an alpha fixture pack and should remain canary for production scans until broader holo-device QA is complete.
- Rollback path: disable `holo_glare_reinspection_v1` in the promoted skill registry and fall back to normal surface uncertainty warnings.
